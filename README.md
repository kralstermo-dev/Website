# Daily Games
> Status: Work In Progress

A collection of games That reset daily, hence the name. this is not supposed to be stolen from anywhere and might be buggy this is made because i was bored and needed a better version for ME, you might find others better so play them instead

Have Fun! And here is the list of games and how to play them:

## Wordle
The Classic, 5 letter word guessing game. You guess any 5 letter word and the letters show info; Green: In the word and right place, Yellow: In the word but not in the right place, gray: not in the word
## Nerdle
A guessing game where you try to guess todays equation. This is how it works: you guess a equation for example: 12+07=19 (it does have to be mathematically be correct) and same rules as wordle: Green: In the equation and right place, Yellow: In the equation but not in the right place, gray: not in the equation
## Flagle
Try to guess the zoomed in flag or try to match the colors of the flag and guess
## Colorfle
Mix three different sizes of colors to try to get the daily color, and with three game modes

Normal: Mix three colors to try to get the daily

Hard: Mix four colors to try to get the daily

Impossible: Try To find the [hex code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_colors)
## Letterle
Wordle but with one letter
## Angle
Try to get the exact angle shown on screen
