A note on the quizzes on this site: they're informal, simplified self-reflection tools - not diagnostic instruments, and not authoritative. They work by asking a handful of branching questions and matching your answers to the closest common label, which means they can easily be wrong, oversimplified, or miss a term entirely. Real identity is more nuanced than any quiz can capture, terminology varies between communities and changes over time, and you're always the only real authority on what fits you. Treat any result as a starting point for reflection or vocabulary, not a verdict - and it's completely fine to use a different label, multiple labels, or none at all.
